Anomaly Detection Engine Architecture

1. Scikit-Learn Isolation Forest

Constructs an ensemble of isolation decision trees across multiple feature dimensions (amount, category index, day of week, day of month). Points with short path lengths are mathematically isolated as anomalies.

2. Statistical IQR & Z-Score Filter

Calculates upper Tukey bounds (Q3 + 1.5 * IQR) for each distinct expense category. Catches sudden spikes that exceed category norms even if transaction timing is ordinary.

3. Duplicate & Velocity Shield

Cross-references identical timestamps, merchant descriptors, and dollar amounts within 72-hour windows to identify unintended duplicate charges.
